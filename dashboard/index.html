    Just after Record, and again just before Stop, clap 2–3 times about a second apart, close to all the boards and the same distance from each: their clocks are lined up on those claps (task align). Each board's LED flashes when it hears one.

    Boards' flash: each board records on its own (LED dim red) and keeps going if you disconnect or carry it off. Upload runs at ~12 KB/s per board, all boards at once: a full 8 MB takes about 11 minutes.
