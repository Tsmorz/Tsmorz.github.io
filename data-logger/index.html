      Settings

      Mouse saved on the board

      Switch the mouse off on boards you are only recording, so they don't move your cursor.

      IMU ranges saved on the board

      A narrower range resolves smaller motions but clips faster ones.

      Clock sync radio, saved on the boards

        One board broadcasts its clock over Bluetooth and every other board records, about once a second, what time it was on both. task align then lines the boards up on the source's clock to about a microsecond, with no clap needed and no error from distance. Claps still work as a cross-check.

        Each board remembers its role, so a flash recording after a restart still syncs. With no source, the others only listen in short bursts.

        Sleep saved on the board

        Off: only a USER press wakes a sleeping board.

        Lights

        Overrides the status LED (blue advertising, green connected) until the next connect, disconnect or clap.

        Voice saved on the board

        Connect a board to set up its voice commands.

          Every board in earshot hears every command, so each board obeys only the ones ticked here. "Capture complete" and "power down now" act only when said twice within 4 s; the LED blinks amber after the first. Every command heard is recorded as an event, acted on or not, so false triggers show in the data.

          A board needs firmware built with a trained model to listen: until then, record clips and background audio to train one. See docs/voice-commands.md.

                Plug the board into this computer by USB, press Flash and pick its programmer (XIAO nRF54LM20A CMSIS-DAP). Takes about 15 s. It erases the whole chip first, so the board's settings and Bluetooth bonds go: if this computer paired with it before, remove it from the Bluetooth settings before adding it again.                 The last pair that flashed a board here is kept in this browser and chosen when this opens, so a broken board can be flashed without the files; drop new ones to replace either. A Bluetooth update that succeeds moves the kept firmware on to that version.
